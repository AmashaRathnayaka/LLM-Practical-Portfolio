# Final Reflection – PUSL3205

## Large Language Models and Generative AI
### Individual Practical Portfolio

## 1. Introduction

Throughout the PUSL3205 Large Language Models and Generative AI module, I gained practical experience in developing and experimenting with different artificial intelligence techniques. The laboratory activities helped me understand how AI systems work, starting from sentiment analysis and progressing towards Transformer models, fine-tuning, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and tool-using AI agents.

This portfolio documents my practical work, the challenges I encountered, and the knowledge I gained from Lab Sheets 1, 3, 4, 5, and 6.

## 2. Key Learning Outcomes

### Lab Sheet 1 – Sentiment Analysis

In this lab, I learned how Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques can be used to classify text according to sentiment. I gained experience in preparing datasets, training and evaluating a sentiment classification model, and interpreting evaluation results.

This activity helped me understand the importance of data preprocessing, model performance, and evaluation metrics when developing machine learning applications.

### Lab Sheet 3 – Tiny Transformer

This lab introduced me to the architecture and implementation of Transformer models. I explored how a small Transformer model can be trained and evaluated using text data.

I gained a better understanding of tokenization, model training, training loss, and the role of Transformers in modern language processing applications.

### Lab Sheet 4 – LoRA Fine-Tuning

In this lab, I explored Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA), a parameter-efficient fine-tuning technique used to adapt pretrained language models.

I learned how fine-tuning can improve a model's performance for specific tasks without retraining all its parameters. I also gained experience in configuring LoRA, preparing training data, evaluating model outputs, and saving model files.

### Lab Sheet 5 – Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

This lab helped me understand how language models can retrieve information from external documents to generate more relevant responses.

I worked with PDF documents, text splitting, embeddings, and vector databases to develop a RAG-based chatbot.

Through this activity, I learned how document retrieval can support question-answering systems and how the quality of retrieved information affects the generated responses.

### Lab Sheet 6 – Tool-Using AI Agent

In the final lab, I developed an AI agent capable of performing different tasks using external tools.

I implemented calculator functionality, conversation memory using SQLite, and document retrieval using RAG. I also integrated Google Calendar to retrieve scheduled events and create new events with user confirmation.

This lab helped me understand how different AI components can be combined to build a more interactive and functional application.

## 3. Challenges Encountered

During the laboratory activities, I encountered several technical challenges, including software installation problems, dependency issues, model configuration difficulties, and runtime errors.

One significant challenge occurred during Lab Sheet 6, when I experienced difficulties installing the required software locally. I used Google Colab as an alternative environment to complete the implementation.

I also encountered issues with runtime restarts, missing variables, and Google Calendar authentication. These problems required me to review the code, execute notebook cells in the correct order, and troubleshoot authentication errors.

Working through these challenges improved my problem-solving skills and helped me understand the importance of testing and debugging AI applications.
